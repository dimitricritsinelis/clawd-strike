4. Use [.claude/skills/map-polish/SKILL.md](.claude/skills/map-polish/SKILL.md) to distinguish document work, construction and validation. A complete build includes its declared shared interfaces and bounded in-game evidence. For the approved full-map task, follow the [canonical queue and readiness contract](docs/map-design/construction/README.md#approved-whole-map-queue-and-readiness) and continue automatically after each area’s end review and corrections.
5. Old KEEP labels and frontage exemptions do not freeze weak render-only content. Use the exact BZ-04 dispositions and producer/registry replacement steps. Preserve existing unrelated work, protected gameplay, source provenance and real validation gates.
6. Do not improvise composition, add unscheduled decoration, use AI images as construction proof, or report document approval as implemented quality. User-rejected visuals remain rejected even if numerical checks pass.

7. Implement the approved complete area first, review once at the end, then make targeted corrections. Reuse shared assets and the existing capture runner; do not add mid-build reviews or recurring approval cycles. Follow the current area-specific installation phase, including R7 B’s `B-05-finish-only` work and reuse of unchanged B-04 local gateway caps until their scheduled full-map replacement. Historical PROPOSED labels do not request new approval.

Use the durable commands in the construction README. Freeze the area handoff hash at task start; if its revision changes during implementation, resolve that specific change before applying assets. Technical fixture tests are part of construction, not an aesthetic review gate.
