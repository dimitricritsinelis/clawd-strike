# R2.2 corrections following independent recheck

R2.1 received game-studio approval. Architecture identified a north/south drawing conversion defect and obsolete Service North relationship notes. Engineering identified a 180-degree free-model placement error. The complete prior reports and manifest remain alongside this record.

- The shared drawing conversion now maps positive street offset south at north boundaries, west at east boundaries, north at south boundaries and east at west boundaries. Four fixed world-coordinate fixtures run before generation. Staff connections, chambers and recessed fields regenerate on the correct side of their walls.
- `buildings[]` is the sole building-use/relationship authority. Obsolete parcel room copies and `propertyRelationships` were removed. A ninth negative fixture rejects reintroduced competing relationship data.
- All 19 roof bundles use raw placement yaw 180 with the baked `(eastOffset, height, northOffset)` glTF frame. The existing loader adds 180, producing an identity world orientation. Shared environment and all 15 skyline placements use the same explicit frame. The legacy runtime transform remains unchanged.
- `verify-transforms.ts` passes asymmetric probes through the actual `buildAuthoredPlacements` and `buildSectionModels` functions. It verifies all 19 bundles and 25 zone origins and rejects every former yaw-zero bundle fixture.

No game source, map specification, gameplay geometry or asset export was changed by these corrections. Actual exported-mesh, integration, gameplay, performance and user-art acceptance remain implementation-stage conditions.
