# BZ-04 | Materials, assemblies and workmanship

All dimensions are metres. Area schedules supply the unique instance dimensions, absolute positions, purpose and receiver. These details supply the construction recipe; they never authorize another opening, prop or repeated decoration. Named material slots are shared across areas. This is virtual scenery: no live interior access, structural certification or physical construction is implied.

**Current R6 authority:** [craftsmanship.md](craftsmanship.md) and each opening's architecturalDetail/glazingProfile fields override generic finishes below for the named instances. B retains its private material naming, building layout, balcony and roof phase, with user-authorized selected profile changes. The retained Dogleg balcony and Tea seat/shades are complete measured assemblies; the Caravan palm is removed. Other details are instance-bound assemblies, not permission for arbitrary added props. Full export bounds include frames, supports, cloth and foliage. No opaque backing, receiver contact or protected route is removed.

## 1. Material control schedule

The selected R6 material recipes and full owner/area assignments are in `design.json` and the generated [runtime material schedule](runtime-materials.md). That schedule is the numerical authority for source files, desired appearance, bounded paint input, physical UV repeat, crop, normal strength, roughness mode, metalness, retained macro behavior and resolved material/shadow classes. The earlier hand-written tint table is retired because a desired cream swatch is not the multiplier that produces cream from a dark source.

The existing BZ-04 wall/floor aliases, URL texture cache and roof batching already exist. Reuse their infrastructure. Build only the selected recipe or binding changes required by the frozen area handoff; do not repeat the original alias installation or change generic material paths used by unrelated areas. Preserve original texture files, URLs, licences and checksums. The exact source remains explicit even where its older alias name describes the wrong physical surface.

Apply the selected material recipe once. A painted material uses either a bounded neutral source field multiplied by the desired linear vertex paint, or a baked desired paint with white vertex color; the schedule selects the path. Never multiply desired paint into both. Source albedo is sRGB; normal and packed ARM are non-color. A constant roughness disconnects the scan input, while a factor explicitly multiplies it. Nonmetallic surfaces disconnect ARM Metallic and set metalness to zero. Preserve the separate named iron/brass values.

Color calibration is a render-only finish and cannot change floor grade, silhouettes or colliders. The material samples establish the selected design recipe, not final game appearance. The first area end review checks the same source/formula under neutral and shipped lighting, including horizontal paving against vertical walls and painted joinery in shade. Keep the existing runtime macro layer; do not add a second full-wall mask because a review coupon omitted it.

Paving uses a shared physical stone-unit scale through connected public routes and a separate rougher service family. Change at a real court/service threshold with a flush joint. One-piece sills and straight trim use the selected mortar-free crop; masonry wall courses and deliberately assembled arch voussoirs retain coherent joints. Domestic linen uses the selected fine physical repeat of the existing woven source, never the leather substrate. No new source is inferred from a conditional candidate list.

Wood UVs follow the length of each member. Avoid mapping one giant masonry repeat across door straps, narrow trim or every exposed edge. Stone joints remain at a consistent physical scale; adjacent slabs may share continuous world UVs, but repair fields and property boundaries have the explicit extents shown in the design.

The Project-Original textile atlas and rug albedo are allowed only for their scheduled cloth parts. Sources: `apps/client/public/assets/textures/environment/bazaar/textiles/sources.json`; `project_original/levantine_rug_albedo_v1.jpg`; existing `props/textile_booth/booth_albedo.png`, `booth_normal.png`, `booth_roughness.png`. Read these sources without rebuilding or overwriting the original booth. Use an embedded named material with metallic 0, roughness 0.86 and unmodified albedo; map the rug at 1.20 m per repeat, crop at the edge and preserve the weave. No `ph_dirty_carpet` utility scan for decorative rugs. Project-original procedural assets retain their recorded provenance; they do not claim to be licensed photographic scans. Deriving a bounded paint or UV crop preserves the original source and its checksum.

## 3. Activity recipes

An activity group is one designed assembly, not a licence to scatter extra objects. The area table fixes its ID, opening, bbox, trade, sign and material accent. The complete instanceLayout.parts list in the area source and generated sheet is the final arrangement. The recipes below define the assembly family; never add their contents a second time. Counter carcasses are custom joinery following opening.shopfront dimensions, while goods retain their fixed sizes. The recipe fits inside that box; fixed-size stock is not scaled arbitrarily. A group requiring a larger box is a design conflict. Elements below 2.2 m project at most 0.30 toward the street; deeper contents occupy the specified closed recess behind the face. Keep all door service floors and open transition volumes clear.

## 4. Workmanship and wear

Dress stone arrises 0.006..0.012, rubble arrises 0.010..0.018, timber 0.003..0.006; clamp to one-quarter of the shortest member dimension. Bevel inside the designed silhouette. Small edge losses stay within the original solid; bearing planes remain flat. Smooth arch silhouettes and finished cloth edges are required even when the broad form is simple.

Plaster has low-contrast grain and named repairs, not full-height black grunge. Added wall dust is mostly within 0..0.35 above the local floor. Hand polish is 0.90..1.40 on the served jamb; scuffs are 0..0.60 beside a store door. Do not stain quiet upper fields except a specifically scheduled spout/vent source or sun exposure.

Use only the scheduled contactBand and surfaceTreatments. Contact tint follows the actual floor profile. Insert mesh rows at floor, floor+0.12 and floor+0.18 before baking it, with white vertices at and above+0.18; otherwise interpolation on a tall quad would spread the tint up the wall. White vertices remain unchanged. There is no procedural repair or chip distribution. If a treatment has a polygon, use its stored vertices. Peak added alpha: dust 0.12, damp 0.12, dye 0.12, polish 0.08. Edges feather within the polygon. No sinusoidal bands, visible mesh-grid pattern, regular stripes or repeated patch silhouette. A plain surface with the correct PBR response is preferable to a visibly artificial wear overlay. Exact microvertex positions are bounded workmanship; the composition, footprint, source of wear and amount are design decisions.

## 5. Budget and validation

Per-section material/triangle allowances are design ceilings listed in `design.json`; they include facade shells, groups and fixtures. Reusable roof bundles have separate bounds and allocations in roof-bundles.md. The required static roof batching and the combined global allocation are explicit in roofRenderingContract. Reuse materials and source textures; do not embed a unique scan per patch or instance. No extra real-time light per lantern. Architectural details cast shadows only where their silhouette matters; tiny hardware and wear do not.

The global release limits remain those in `apps/client/scripts/lib/performanceAcceptance.mjs`: desktop 1,500 draw calls, 2.2 million triangles and 12.5 ms CPU median at 1440 x 900; mobile 500 draws, 1.3 million triangles and 30 FPS, with the existing baseline-regression checks. A design allocation is not a measured result. See [integration.md](integration.md) for the required in-game proof.

## 7. Building and small-object construction standards

The building register is designed before its opening list. Structural bay edges, axes, floor levels and entrance hierarchy control the elevation; the opening-to-room association verifies that design. Use the untextured building sheet to check composition before materials or wear. A narrow retaining spine or terminal wall is a BC-01 boundary assembly, not an inhabited building. BC-01 uses the exact bay edges in the source, 0.32-wide engaged piers at the wall plane, recessed fields 0.045 behind it, a 0.60 base and a 0.50 upper band. End half-piers are clipped inside the exact solid span and never enter a route mouth. Graded bases use the explicitly declared raking datum; the existing tread/nosing profile overlaps their foot in the drawing. It adds no route obstruction or filler openings.

An SD-11-CIVIC recess is a deliberate part of the whole gate/loggia composition. Its opaque stone dado rises to the printed 1.10 datum, with a fixed timber screen and opaque plaster back above. An arched door uses its declared lower leaf and fixed shaped transom. No builder supplies an unlisted counter or treats either assembly as a new passage.

All activity z-coordinates start at the group box's minimum z, normally the finished deck at floor+0.04; plant troughs start on the actual floor. Box bounds describe final silhouettes, including rims, ties and handles. Pottery uses a continuous curved profile, at least 32 radial segments, a finished 0.015..0.020 rim and a grounded foot ring. Cup and jar openings have interior depth or a proper lid. Cloth and rugs have thickness, bound edges and restrained curvature within their box. Rolled rugs show a finished rolled end; sacks have a flattened supported base. Timber frames have grounded ends and actual bearing joints. No visible primitive is left as an undecorated construction cube merely because its bounding box is correct.

The two embedded plant materials and contained soil have explicit PBR values in materialRuntimeContract. Each plant stays within its three-dimensional silhouette box, rooted at the soil plane. Each leaf has a tapered curved profile, with three longitudinal segments. These small objects use project-original geometry; they do not introduce external assets or new collision.
