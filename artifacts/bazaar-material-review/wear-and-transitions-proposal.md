## Source-to-palette calibration proposal

Keep the founding reference primary for material relationships, warmth and trade color. Its strongly warm light is a look reference, not a pixel chart to multiply into every albedo. The proposed cream/sand/ochre colors describe desired material appearance; exact runtime tint values remain unfrozen.

1. Choose representative clean field pixels from each inspected source, excluding mortar, deep cavities, tears and edge shadows. Convert every source pixel and desired target from sRGB to linear once. Calculate a robust central field color in linear space, not the mean of gamma-encoded JPEG values. Keep whole-texture dark/light percentiles as a check against clipping and excess contrast.
2. For a plain source-times-tint surface, the initial per-channel multiplier is desiredLinear / representativeSourceLinear. Evaluate that multiplier on the entire source before accepting it. If a dark source demands unsupported above-one multipliers or clips pale stones, replace it with the inspected quieter/lighter shared source; do not force pale sand out of the dark beige scan through global exposure. Normal and roughness response must be checked independently.
3. For paint or lime-coated material, the existing R5 bounded formula is the relevant model: finalLinear = paintLinear × ((1 − grainMix) + grainMix × sourceLinear). Existing cloth uses grainMix 0.12 and warm timber 0.28. A desired final target is therefore not identical to paintSrgb; solve paintLinear from the representative bounded source term, then inspect highlights, seams and normals. Extending that formula to a wall is a proposed material-profile decision, not an already implemented wall behavior. Preserve original scans and provenance.
4. Show the calibrated source on a measured wall, sill, paving patch and cloth/door sample under fixed neutral daylight. Include a known neutral/cream comparison patch in the review only. Then inspect the same materials in the existing shipped lighting without changing exposure, fog or grading. Compare at player distance and close range: the broad field should be sun-faded and warm; shadows should retain cooler separation and surface detail. A raw albedo thumbnail or diagram fill cannot establish that result.
5. Add only the named local wear after the clean base is understood. Compare source-only versus source-plus-authored-wear at those receivers. Retain inherited stone variation and appropriate sanding; remove duplicate all-over dirt or repeated edge bands. Record accepted tint/formula/repeat/normal/roughness values and exact source hashes in the eventual implementation handoff after approval.
